# How to Clear Cache on iOS (Safari/Chrome)

## Method 1: Clear Safari Cache (Recommended)

1. **Open Settings** on your iPhone/iPad
2. Scroll down and tap **Safari**
3. Scroll down and tap **Clear History and Website Data**
4. Tap **Clear History and Data** to confirm
5. **Close Safari completely** (swipe up from bottom, swipe Safari away)
6. **Reopen Safari** and visit the site again

## Method 2: Clear Cache for Specific Website

1. **Open Safari** and go to the website
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow) at the bottom
3. Scroll down and tap **Request Desktop Website** (toggle it on/off)
4. Or tap **Add to Home Screen** then remove it
5. **Close and reopen Safari**

## Method 3: Hard Refresh (Quick Fix)

1. **Open Safari** and go to the website
2. **Tap and hold the refresh button** (circular arrow) in the address bar
3. Tap **Reload Without Content Blockers**
4. Or **close Safari completely** and reopen it

## Method 4: Clear Chrome Cache on iOS

1. **Open Chrome** app
2. Tap the **three dots** (⋮) in the bottom right
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Tap **Privacy**
5. Tap **Clear Browsing Data**
6. Select **Cached Images and Files**
7. Tap **Clear Browsing Data**
8. **Close Chrome completely** and reopen it

## Method 5: Private/Incognito Mode (Test Without Cache)

1. **Open Safari** or **Chrome**
2. Tap the **tabs icon** (two squares)
3. Tap **Private** (Safari) or **New Incognito Tab** (Chrome)
4. Visit the website - this will load fresh without cache

## Method 6: Restart Your Device

1. **Hold the power button** until "slide to power off" appears
2. **Slide to power off**
3. **Wait 30 seconds**
4. **Hold the power button** to turn it back on
5. **Open Safari/Chrome** and visit the site

## Quick Test: Add Version Parameter

To force a cache refresh, you can add `?v=2` to the URL:
- `https://mridultyagi687.github.io/friendly-friends-app-full/?v=2`

This tricks the browser into thinking it's a new page.

---

**Note:** After clearing cache, the website should load with the latest code. If issues persist, try Method 5 (Private/Incognito mode) to confirm it's a cache issue.
